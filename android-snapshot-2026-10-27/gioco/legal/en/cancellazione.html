Account and data deletion

1. In the app

On the flower selection screen, at the bottom, tap «Delete account» and confirm. The data saved in the app (email, gender, acceptance date and preferences) is deleted immediately.

2. By email

If you cannot use the app, write to emmanuele@aivevia.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject «Account deletion». We will reply within 30 days.

3. What is deleted

Email, gender, terms acceptance date and preferences.

4. Uninstalling

Uninstalling the app also deletes the data saved inside it.
